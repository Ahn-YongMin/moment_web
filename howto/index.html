This page is for App Review. Moment has no login, no subscriptions, and no user accounts. All image processing runs on-device.

1. Moment?

Moment is a film scanner app that turns developed film negatives into digital photos on your smartphone. Target users: people who shoot or keep analog film and want a simple way to digitize it without a dedicated scanner.

2. How to use (reviewer steps)

  1. Open Moment (no account required).
  2. Tap Choose from gallery and import the sample negative below, or tap Take a photo to photograph film against a bright light.
  3. Run image processing to convert the negative into a positive-looking photo.
  4. (Optional) Open color adjustments and move sliders (gamma, contrast, saturation, temperature, etc.).
  5. Save the result as JPEG or PNG to your gallery.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph developed film.
  • Photo Library — to import film images and save results.

We do not request App Tracking Transparency for advertising tracking prompts beyond standard ad SDK behavior as configured.

5. External services

  • Google AdMob — banner ads only.
  • No backend server for photos. Images are not uploaded to our servers.
  • No authentication, payments, or cloud AI image APIs.

6. Regional differences

App features are the same worldwide. Ad creatives may vary by region (AdMob fill).

현상된 필름(네거티브)을 스마트폰에서 디지털 사진으로 바꾸는 필름 스캐너 앱입니다. 전용 스캐너 없이 촬영하거나 갤러리에서 가져와 색을 복원하고 저장할 수 있습니다.

2. 사용 방법 (심사 순서)

  1. 앱을 실행합니다 (계정 없음).
  2. 갤러리에서 선택으로 아래 샘플 이미지를 가져오거나, 촬영으로 밝은 조명 앞의 필름을 찍습니다.
  3. 이미지 처리를 실행해 네거티브를 변환합니다.
  4. (선택) 색감 조정에서 감마·대비·채도 등 슬라이더를 조절합니다.
  5. JPEG 또는 PNG로 앨범에 저장합니다.
